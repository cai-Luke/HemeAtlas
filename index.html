About HemeAtlas
What is this?

HemeAtlas is a curated reference library of peripheral blood cell morphology images for hematology education. This is a working document — a more complete methods summary and acknowledgments will be added here in a future update.

Datasets & Attribution

Cell images courtesy of CellWiki (a project of LabThings), used for educational purposes.

KU-Optofil PBC Dataset by Yarıkan et al. (2026), licensed under CC BY 4.0. DOI: 10.1038/s41597-026-06761-y
